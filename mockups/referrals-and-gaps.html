Referrals, tiers, and what is still missing

Three ways to make the database know who sent you whom, plus the honest list of what the Hub has that this dashboard does not. First, the thing you need to know: nowhere in the Hub records who referred a contact. The idea exists in three places and every one of them is empty.

So the system knows three people arrived by referral and cannot tell you from whom. The fix is one column — referred_by on agent_contacts, pointing at another contact. Everything below is built on that single field.

Three ways to show it

Option AOn the record

A Referrals block inside the contact sheet. Referred by at the top with one bubble, Sent you underneath with a bubble for each person they introduced. Click any bubble to open that record. The count sits next to their name everywhere else in the app.

Option BA rail on the list

The bubbles ride on the row in the main list, and anyone who has referred you sorts to the top no matter what tier they are. You see your referrers before you see anyone else, every time you open Database, without opening a single record.

Option CA referrer board

Its own card: everyone who has ever sent you someone, ranked by how many, with the money those introductions produced. The only one that tells you what a referrer is worth, which is what decides who gets the closing gift.

Tiers and the business toggle

Three changes you asked for, shown together. +A above A. D means do not contact and those records drop out of every list until you ask for them. And a Relationship / Business toggle so agents, vendors and partners live in the same database without cluttering the people you farm.

What the Hub has that this does not

The Hub carries 35 panels. What we have built covers eight tabs each side. This is the difference, taken from the panel list in the live page rather than from memory.

Which one, and what I would do first

For referrals I would build A and B together — they are the same field shown twice, and neither works alone. A is where you record it, B is where it changes your day. C comes later, once there are enough referrals to rank; with three referral-sourced contacts and no referrer named, a board would have one row on it.

Before any of it: the referred_by column. Without it none of these three can hold a single piece of real data, and the three contacts you already have marked as referrals stay anonymous.
