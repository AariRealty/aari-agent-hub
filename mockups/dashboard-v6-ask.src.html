The actual emoji, and which one asks

You were right and I was wrong. You sent me ⚙️ and 📧️ the colour emoji, and I built the black-and-white characters that merely look like them. These are the real ones, the same characters you typed, so the bar below is the bar you were picturing. Every phone also carries the fills chain you picked.

The two you offered for Ask are both here, plus one more. You asked which aligns better. My answer is under the three, but the short version is that ❓️ asks a question and 🙋️ asks a person, and Ask is a person, so the hand is closer to the truth than the question mark.
One thing you should decide with your eyes open. Colour emoji bring colour. 🙋️ is a yellow face and ❓️ is red; the house rule has been no yellow or gold anywhere, and that rule is the reason the Hub looks the way it does. It is your rule and your call, I am not going to pretend the conflict is not there, and the third option below is what the row looks like if you want the emoji shapes without breaking it.

What I can and cannot check from here

The screenshots above are real, this machine draws colour emoji through Noto, so the yellow, the red and the blue are genuinely there. What I cannot show you is your phone: Apple draws all four of these characters differently from Noto, and 🙋️ most of all. The shapes and the colour families will hold; the exact faces will not.
