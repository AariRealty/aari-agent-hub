Three more, built around the bubbles

You liked the attendee faces sitting next to the attend button, so all three of these lead with them. Same six entries at the real card width, and every control works — click a face row and your own bubble joins the stack.

Option DFaces beside the button

Closest to what you already liked. The faces sit immediately left of the attend button in a fixed column, and an entry nobody has joined yet shows a single dashed circle rather than the words "no one yet" — so the bubbles line up down the card whether anyone has said yes or not.

Option EFaces lead the row

Turns the bubbles into the left rail. Who is going is the first thing you see on every row, the title sits beside it, and the attend control shrinks to a quiet Join so the faces carry the row instead of competing with a button.

Option FOne combined pill

Faces, count and the toggle become a single control. Tap the pill and your face drops into the stack and the whole thing goes dark. Nothing can look half-empty, because there is only ever one object on the right.

Which one

I would pick F. It keeps the bubbles you liked, and it is the only one of the three where an event with nobody attending and an event with three cannot look like different kinds of row — the pill is one shape, always. D is the safest and the closest to what is on the dashboard now. E reads beautifully when several people are going and thinly when they are not, so it suits a calendar with more classes on it than yours currently has.
