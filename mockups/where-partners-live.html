Which one, and the answer to your question

Yes, they should be under Database — you are right, and the duplication above proves it. A title company you refer clients to is a relationship: it has a number, a last contact, a person who answers, and deals that came out of it. That is exactly what a contact record is for. A link to the Lee County Property Appraiser is none of those things, and putting it in a contact database would be strange. So the line is not Resources versus Database — it is people and companies versus web pages.

I would build C. It is B underneath, which is the correct data model, plus the one thing B costs you: on a call with a buyer you want the title company and the flood map on the same screen, not in two tabs. C gives you that without a second copy, because the Resources tab reads the same rows rather than keeping its own.

B is the honest smaller build if you would rather not maintain a second view — it fixes the duplication and nothing more. A I would not take: it is the only one that leaves Trinity Hansen recorded twice, and that is the problem you spotted.
