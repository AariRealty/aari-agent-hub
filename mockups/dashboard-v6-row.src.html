You are right, and here is why

Option A said the labels go to one line. They did not, and the reason is a real mistake rather than a matter of taste.

The labels have a line break written into them. The markup builds convos<br>today, and I tried to flatten it with a CSS rule that stops text wrapping. That rule has no effect on a <br>, which is an instruction to break rather than a wrap. So the row you approved as one line has been rendering as two the whole time, and I told you it shipped.

The reason it is not a one-word fix is the thing you caught earlier: three a day and three a week both read as "3", so the window has to be on every figure. Dropping the second word would save the line and put that confusion straight back. All three below keep every window word and every track. They differ in where the window goes.
