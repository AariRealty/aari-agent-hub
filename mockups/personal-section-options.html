Three simpler ways to show Personal

Same five answers — in the market, type, stage, tier, language — with the black pill and the grey Change on every line taken out. All three still edit: click a value and the choices appear, pick one and they fold away again.

Option APlain values

The answer sits on the right as plain text with a faint dashed underline, the way a form that has already been filled in reads. Click the value itself to change it — no separate link.

Personal
Option BOne line

The whole block becomes a single row of small chips, with anything unanswered showing as a dashed outline. Five rows become one. Edit opens the fields; done closes them again.

Personal
Option CTwo columns

A small label above each answer, two to a line, so five fields take three lines instead of five. Scans like a record card and leaves room for the rest of the sheet.

Personal

Which one

I would pick C. It is the only one that actually shortens the sheet — five lines become three, which matters because Contact, Household, Activity and Tags all sit underneath it. The labels stay small and grey so your eye lands on the answers, and every value is still one click from changing.

A is the smallest change from what is there now and the safest if you want the panel to keep reading top to bottom. B is the tidiest to look at, but it hides the field names, so on a record where three of the five are blank you would be reading dashed outlines and guessing which is which.
