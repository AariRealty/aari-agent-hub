The last set was wrong and you caught both halves of it. I drew a different card, the dark hour rail that lives on the old agent grid, and presented it as your Today. Nothing in the Hub was changed, only the mockup was wrong. Below, the banner and the queue are reproduced exactly as they are today, down to the buttons and the gap pills. The only difference between the three is where the new work attaches, marked in red on each.

On the black. You were half right and I should be precise. The number banner is already black in the Hub and always has been, that part is yours and I did not touch it. What I did was make the whole card black and turn your day into a to-do list on it. That was new, it was not asked for, and it is gone.

All three are drawn on Thursday 27 August, the day your saved pop-by day, your filming day, the daily conversation and the daily post all land together. The three names are the real queue, and the video bank is really zero.
