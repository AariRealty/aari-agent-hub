Aari Hub · moving between tabs

Swipe across, three ways

All three are live. Drag them. They work with a finger on your phone and with a mouse here, and each one moves between Today, Deals, People and Money. Let go halfway and it settles to whichever side you were closer to.

The greeting and the tab bar never move, only what sits between them. Tapping the bar still works and slides to the tab you tapped, so nothing you do today stops working.

I took the idea from your prompt, not the code. That one is React with Tailwind, video textures and a pure black background, and it draws credit cards. None of that belongs in the Hub, which is one plain HTML file on a warm paper ground. What is worth keeping is the part that makes it feel good: a continuous drag rather than a jump, real momentum, and depth so you can see where the next thing is. B is the one that takes that furthest.

Mobile only. Nothing here would be built above 720px. On a desktop the tabs stay exactly as they are, because there is no swipe on a mouse worth designing for and the rail already shows every tab at once.

One thing to feel for while you drag. All three carry the same rule: a swipe that travels less than about a fifth of the screen springs back rather than changing tab. Without it, a scroll that drifts sideways throws you onto another tab, which is the single most annoying thing a phone can do. Try a small lazy drag on each and see it refuse.

And what I have not done. None of this is in your Hub yet. It is a page on its own so you can test the feel before I touch the navigation, because swapping how the tabs work is the one change that could break every screen at once.
