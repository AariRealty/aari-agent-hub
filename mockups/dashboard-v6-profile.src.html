A real profile, and the tabs the phone never shows you

You were right that the last three were just the bottom bar again. This is the thing you actually asked for: somewhere an agent edits their photo, licence, anniversary and address, chooses what they see, and reaches the parts of the Hub the phone hides.

On what is missing: the phone shows five tab icons and only the sections of the tab you are standing on. The computer shows the whole set in its rail. For an agent that is 9 sections, of which the phone shows one or two at a time; on your broker side it is 20. In all three, the last icon opens that whole map, and Reach moves into it.

Every value below is the real one, and the empty ones are really empty.
  • Licence BK3530153 and your phone, email and counties are what the Hub holds.
