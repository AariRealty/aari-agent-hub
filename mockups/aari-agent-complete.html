Say each thing once

You are seeing the same things over and over because the dashboard genuinely does repeat itself. I counted every figure on your agent view: ten facts, said twenty-six times. Adding cards was never going to fix that. Below is the count, then three ways to make it complete and shorter at the same time — because most of what needs cutting is a duplicate, not a fact.

The five dials are the worst of it — Contacts, Convos, Appts, Contract, Closed. Every one of the five repeats something said elsewhere on the same screen, and together they take a whole band. The three headline numbers do the same. Cutting both loses nothing.

Everything you have said you like stays in all three: your picture, the doughnut, the income bars, and the fifteen-days-left bar. What comes out is duplication, and what goes in is the two things genuinely missing — the documents your file needs and your onboarding.

Version 1Each thing once

The smallest change that removes every repeat. The dial band and the headline numbers come off. My listings and Your open file merge into one card called My work. Path to first close is replaced by Getting set up, which reads the real ten onboarding items instead of five hardcoded steps. Everything else stays where it is.

Which one

Version 2. It is the one that is complete without being long. Seven cards, every fact once, both missing things in, and the two merges it makes are the two that were always slightly odd — the doughnut was measuring the bars beside it, and the documents belong to the file they block. It loses nothing you said you liked.

Version 1 is the safe one if you would rather change as little as possible. It only deletes; it does not merge anything. Take it if Version 2 feels like too much movement at once.

Version 3 is the one I would not take yet, and it is the shortest. It fits a laptop screen with no scrolling, which is genuinely valuable — but it puts your listings, your open file and its six missing documents in one card, and the day someone has four listings and two files that card becomes the thing you have to scroll instead of the page. It is the right answer for a brand-new agent and the wrong one for Milennys.

What none of them fix. The doughnut still reads 6% of goal, Behind for you and would read 0% for everyone else, because realty_agent_goals holds one row — yours. Every other agent is being shown a percentage of a target they never set. That is a data problem, not a layout one, and it sits under all three.
