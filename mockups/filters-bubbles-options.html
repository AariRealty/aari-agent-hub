Filters on a phone

Same five filters, same behavior — pick several inside one filter, and they stack across filters. What changes is how much of the screen they take before you can see the list.

Why it looks like that now. The rule that puts them two to a row lets each one grow to fill the leftover space, so the first pill in each row expands and pushes the second down. They end up one per line no matter the width.

Which one

I would pick C. It costs one line forever — you have five filters today and you will want more once Business and Scorecard fill in, and A and B both grow taller as that happens while C does not. Sliding the active ones to the front means the filter you are working in is always the first thing under your thumb.

A is the shortest card of the three and the best if you mostly search by name and filter rarely, but it costs a tap before you can see what the filters even are. B is the smallest change and the easiest to read at a glance, and it is the one to take if you want the phone to look like the desktop.
