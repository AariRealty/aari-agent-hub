Where the section tabs go

The five main tabs stay exactly as they are — Today, Deals, People, Money, Reach. This is only about the row underneath them, the one that says Team · Roster · Recruits · Onboarding · Accounts. Three places it could live. Tap around inside any of the three phones.

Why the current spot is the weak one

Right now the row sits between the greeting and the numbers, at 90px on a 390px phone. It belongs to neither — it reads as part of the greeting block rather than as navigation, which is most likely why it stopped registering as tabs at all.

And the two levels are 674px apart: the sections are at the top of the screen, the main tabs are at the very bottom under your thumb. Changing tab is a thumb tap; changing section within it is a reach to the top. Every option below fixes one of those two things.

Option 1Welded to the top

The row moves up against the logo bar, edge to edge with a hairline above and below, and stays pinned there while you scroll. It stops looking like part of the greeting and starts looking like what it is. The greeting then sits directly on top of your content.

Option 3The title is the tab

No row at all. The heading becomes the picker — it says which section you are in, and tapping it drops the list of the five. Fewest things on the screen by a distance, and the section you are in is stated in words rather than inferred from a highlight.

Quietest screen, and the page tells you where you are. Costs a tap every time, and you cannot see the other four sections without opening it.

Which one I would take

Option 2. The complaint has been that the sections do not register, and the reason is that they are 674px away from the tabs they belong to. Putting them on top of the tab bar makes the relationship physical — you tap Deals, and the sections of Deals appear one line above your thumb, in the same block, without your eyes leaving the bottom of the screen. It is also the only option where changing section costs no reach at all, and it gives back the most content height at the top.

Option 1 is the safe one and the one that keeps the phone and the desktop identical, which you have asked for before. Take it if the sections disappearing when you scroll is what bothers you — pinned, they never leave. Option 3 is the one for a loud day: one heading, no row, the page says “Team” and nothing else competes. The cost is real though — you cannot see that Roster and Recruits exist until you open it, and discovering things by seeing them is how you have used this so far.
