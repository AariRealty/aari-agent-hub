The day's number, three ways

You are right that the row was wrong. Three of its four figures were typed into the file by hand, and the three denominators it did show were whole year totals, so the row read the same on the 2nd of January as it will on the 30th of December. These three all read from your saved goal instead, and all three reset at midnight.

Before the pictures, two things the database says that shape every one of them.

What the row needsWhat the Hub actually holds
A daily conversation target Yours is 3 a day. It is not stored, it is arithmetic on your saved row: $150,000 at $8,750 net a closing is 18 closings, 52 appointments, 650 conversations, 14 a week over 48 weeks, 3 over 5 prospecting days.
A daily appointment target There is not one. 52 appointments across 240 prospecting days is a fifth of an appointment a day. Printing "0 of 1" would be a target you never set.
A daily closing target There is not one. 18 a year is one every three weeks.
Goals for the other seven seats None. realty_agent_goals holds one row, yours, saved 31 July. Alejandro, Alied, Ana, Eileen, Flavia, Milennys and Roosevelt have nothing saved, so their row has no denominator at all.
Today's logged conversations 0. agent_activity holds 3 conversations, all yours, on 1, 3 and 16 August. None today.

Only conversations divide into a day. That is the whole design problem here, and it is why the three below are not three arrangements of the same four columns. Each one answers it differently: A drops to the single number that is genuinely daily, B keeps four steps but makes each one say out loud which window it counts, C stops gauging and starts counting.

Every one of them shows a second state under the phone, which is what the other seven agents will actually see. That state is not a design flourish. Seven of your eight seats have no saved goal, so whichever you pick, most of the brokerage sees the empty version first.

One question I cannot answer for you. Today is a Sunday, and your goal says 5 prospecting days a week. Should Sunday show 0 of 3, or should it say the day is not one of your five? All three are drawn on the working-day reading. Say the word and it becomes a rest day in whichever you pick.

No bar, no denominator, no invented figure. A link to the Goal Engine instead.

Why it works: it is the only one that does not put a yearly number next to a daily one and hope you read the difference. The big figure is today, and it is the size of the thing it is measuring, 3, not 650. Cost: you lose the chain. The row no longer shows that conversations feed appointments feed closings, which is the thing the four columns were for.

Four steps that all say "Not set" is a loud reminder, and an empty row of it every morning may read as nagging.

Why it works: nothing is lost. The chain is still a chain, and the two figures that cannot be daily stop pretending, because the word above them says so. The dark rule under the first step is the only mark that moves day to day. Cost: it is four windows on one row, which is more to read than the row you have now, and three of the four still barely move in a day.

What Ana Puentes sees
No marks yet. Set your income goal and the Hub works out how many a day that is.
Nothing to draw without a target, so the empty state is a sentence, the same as A.

Why it works: three empty circles is a thing you can finish. A bar at 33 percent is a statistic, one of three filled is a task, and it is the only one of the three that will feel different at lunchtime than it did at breakfast. Cost: it only works while the number is small. Roosevelt setting a $400,000 goal would need 8 a day, and eight circles will not fit this row.

What "reset daily" needs underneath, whichever you pick. The count on the phone today comes from a list of names with no date on it, saved in the browser, so it never falls back to zero. It has to become a date-stamped log: a conversation logged today counts today, and tomorrow the day's figure starts at zero while the week and the year keep it. agent_activity already has the occurred_on column that does this, so this is a change to the Hub, not to the database.

And the seven empty seats are the real work. A daily number that reads from a goal is worth nothing to an agent who has not set one. Whichever of these you pick, the useful next step is getting the other seven through the Goal Engine once.
