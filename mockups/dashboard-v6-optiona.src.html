The one new card

Today’s plan, three ways

Same content in all three, and in all three pop-bys are a line the plan owns rather than something I hard code. Today is Monday the 24th, and your row says pop_by_day is 4, so pop-bys land on Thursday. That is why the Monday panel below has no pop-by line and the Thursday one does. Nothing is invented: Monday and Thursday are what the Hub prints today, with the pop-by line added.

What I would build

Shape one, and the reason is not that it is prettiest

Shape one keeps the Also today pattern you already approved and changes only where the schedule is read from, so the visible change on Monday is a card with a new title. Everything risky in this piece of work is underneath: a table for the plan, and the Today tab reading it. Changing the look at the same time would mean that if something comes out wrong, neither of us knows which half did it.

Shape two is the one that answers your actual sentence, if I tell you Wednesdays and Thursdays it would show up, because you can see the whole week without waiting for the day. I would build it second, once the plan is real, as a change to the finished card. Its cost now is that it is a second week strip on a screen that already has one on the calendar, sixty pixels below.

Shape three encodes something true that the Hub keeps trying to teach, that a pop-by is a conversation and filming is not. Its cost is that it splits a four line card into two groups of two, which is heavier furniture than four lines deserve.
