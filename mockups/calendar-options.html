Three ways to show the calendar

Same seven events, same two sources, three different shapes. Everything is live except the dates themselves, which sit on the two Google calendars. Training entries always carry an attendance confirmation; company entries never do.

Option ATimeline

No grid at all. Dates run down a rail in order, grouped by this week, next week, September. Built for the fact that a month here holds seven events, not seventy — a 35-cell grid spends most of its space on empty days.

Which one

I would pick A, the timeline. Seven events do not need thirty-five boxes, and it is the only one of the three that fits the dashboard slot without either shrinking the type or pushing the row taller. B is the better choice if you want the dashboard to answer "what is this week" and you are happy to open a full page for anything further out. C is the most familiar, but at dashboard width the cells get tight and it needs roughly double the height of A.
