The broker side inside hub_next, three ways

Same screen, the Contracts panel, drawn three different ways so the choice is about what it looks like rather than what it costs on paper. Each one is a different answer to the same problem: both modules attach to a sidebar contract hub_next does not have.

Figures are the real book, read from the Aari database today: 64 files, 31 with a contract pointer, 24 through the extractor, 7 with a pointer never run. Flag counts are what the rules produce on the parsed set. File names, client names and addresses are withheld and shown as a middle dot, because this repository is public.

One.Bridge it and inject as is

Synthesise the six things the modules reach for, inject them unchanged, ship today. The panels keep the old Hub's stylesheet, so this is what the broker side would actually look like on switch day. Cheapest by a wide margin and the only one that is reachable this week. The cost is on this screen, not in the schedule.

Two.Bridge it, then remap the stylesheet

Same injected markup, plus a compatibility sheet that points the old class names at the v6 tokens. Colour, type and spacing come right without touching the modules. What stays wrong is structural: the tab strip is still a boxed row rather than the Hub's underline, and the details are still a two column table rather than cards. Roughly a day on top of option one.

What I would pick, and the cost of it

Three is the screen you want. One is the screen you can have this week. I would ship one, then port to three with Contracts first, and skip two entirely.

Two looks like the safe middle and is the worst of the set. It costs a day of work on a stylesheet that exists only to flatter markup you are about to delete, and it lands somewhere that is neither the old Hub nor the new one, which is the hardest place to judge whether the port is finished.

The honest cost of my pick is the first screenshot above. For a period measured in weeks the broker and TC screens will look like the old Hub sitting inside the new one, on your screen and Eileen's, and nobody else's. The agents never see these panels. What you buy is that the switch can be flipped before the port is done, so agents move onto the new Hub without the broker side going dark, which is the failure the whole replace decision was blocked on.
