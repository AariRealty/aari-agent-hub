Moving a card

Every card on the dashboard can be moved, made wider or narrower, or switched off — on the broker side and the agent side, each keeping its own arrangement. Here is the whole thing on a computer, step by step. Nothing you do here can break anything: there is a Reset to default button, and the cards themselves are untouched.

1
Point at a card

Two small buttons appear in its corner — a grip and an ×. That is the whole feature. There is no mode to turn on and nothing to turn off afterwards, so there is no state you can get stuck in.

2
Drag the grip

Press and hold the grip and move the mouse. The card you are dragging goes pale and the others slide out of the way as you pass over them. Let go where you want it. The card is never covered, so you can still read the thing you are moving.

3
Press × to take a card off

It becomes a chip in a strip under the greeting, and one press puts it back. Reset layout appears beside it once anything has moved or been hidden, and disappears again when you are back to the original.

4
On a phone the buttons never hide

There is no pointing at things on a touch screen, so the grip and the × are simply always there. Dragging works with a finger.

On a phone
Nothing to point at
A touch screen has no hover, so the grip and the × are always visible rather than appearing. Everything else is identical.
Without the mouse
Tab and arrow keys
Press Tab until a card’s grip is focused, then ← → or ↑ ↓ walk it through the grid one place at a time. The card keeps focus as it moves, so you can keep going without dragging at all.
Where it is kept
This browser, for now
Your arrangement is saved in the browser you made it in, per role — so the broker layout and the agent layout are separate, and your laptop and your phone each keep their own. Nothing is written back to the Hub yet. Making it follow you between devices is a column on the members table and one save call; say the word.
