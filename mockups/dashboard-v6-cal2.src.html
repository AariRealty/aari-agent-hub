Who is going, and onto your Google

Three more, all built around the two things you asked for: a bubble that says who is attending, simplified, and a way to put the Aari calendar onto your Google Calendar. They differ in where the bubble lives and how loud the Google row is.

Two things the Hub really does hold, and one it does not.

  • The Google connection is built and nobody has used it. There is an agent_google_calendar table with a Google address, a refresh token and a connected date on it. It has zero rows, so no agent is connected, and neither are you. Every option below shows that state honestly rather than pretending it is on.
  • Feeds coming in already work. One subscription is active, Attorney Martinez, In-House Counsel classes, last synced 1:07 AM today, two entries. That is where the Live Class entries come from. Traffic runs into the Hub, nothing runs out to your Google yet.
  • Attendance is not stored anywhere. No table, no column, in the whole database. The bubbles on the card today live in the browser only, and all thirteen entries have an empty list. So every phone below shows nobody attending, because that is the truth. Under each one is a strip showing the same bubble once people start ticking, labelled as a shape, not as data.

On the Google mark. The real one is four colours, one of them yellow. Rather than break the palette I have set it as a plain black tile with a G. Say the word if you would rather have the proper coloured mark and I will treat it as a second agreed exception.
