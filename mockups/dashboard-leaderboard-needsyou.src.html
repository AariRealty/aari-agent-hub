Which two

Leaderboard 3 and Needs you 1.

The leaderboard as it stands has a real defect, not just a style problem: its bars are 100, 25, 25 and 25, so $2,200 draws exactly the same length as $32,000. Any of the three fixes that. I would take 3 because it also fixes the second thing — the card names four agents and you have six seats, so two people who have closed nothing this year are invisible on the one card that is about who is producing. That is the number you would want to act on.

Needs you 1 because half of the current card is spent saying nothing is waiting. Documents says “0 waiting” and Licence checks says “All current”, which is two of four rows telling you there is no news. Dropping empty lanes makes the card shrink on a good day, which is the behaviour you want from something called Needs you.

Needs you 3 is the one to take if you would rather always see all four lanes, so an empty one reads as checked rather than missing. Say two numbers and I will build them.
