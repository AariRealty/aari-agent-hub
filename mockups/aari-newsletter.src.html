What the Hub actually fills

I checked realty_listings rather than assuming.

Listing Spotlight is the one section the Hub can lead on, and it is the section I had left out. You have 7 active listings. The Hub already holds the address, the price and the type for all seven: Lehigh Acres, Port Charlotte, Cape Coral, Montura Ranches, Punta Gorda. Because they are Aari’s own, the permission problem your guide warns about does not arise.
What it cannot fill, and why the cards below have gaps. There is no photograph column on that table at all. No MLS number on any of the seven. No list date, so no days on market. Every showings count is zero. And every note is a migration artefact reading “Migrated from realty_transactions…” rather than a selling point. So the Hub can draft three cards and the agent still writes the “why this one” line, which is the part your guide says actually matters. Four columns would close most of that gap.
Two guardrails, built in rather than written down. Every researched item carries a source pill. A link is required before the section can be marked done, which is your guide’s “please provide sources” with teeth. And choosing another agent’s listing raises a permission tick that blocks the send until it is confirmed. With six agents sending monthly, prose will not hold that; a blocked button will.
The recurring events list is the best idea in your guide and it is in every option below. The farmers market and the first-Friday art walk barely change month to month. You write that list once and confirm it thereafter, which is the single biggest cut to the monthly hour of research.
