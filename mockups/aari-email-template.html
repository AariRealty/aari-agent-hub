Measured off the real site

Thank you for the screenshots — they showed me the site is not what I was building from. Your aari-landing-page skill is out of date. It describes a pure-black page, buttons at a 4px radius, and “black and white only”. The live site is light-dominant with full-bleed black bands, warm creams, fully-rounded pill buttons, and a shallow wave where one band meets the next. That wave is the fun division you have been asking me for twice.

Every colour below was sampled from your screenshots, not chosen by me. #ffffff for the hero and light sections, #f5f4f1 for the card ground, #f5f0e8 for the warm band, #0a0a0a for the black bands, #141210 for the announcement bar and every pill, #f0e8da for cream ink on black, #e7e2d7 for card borders. I also measured the wave: 18–55px of rise on a 1320px-wide capture, which is a gentle ~22px curve at letter width.
#ffffff #f5f4f1 #f5f0e8 #0a0a0a #141210 #f0e8da #e7e2d7
Three ways to divide the sections — the site’s own wave, the site’s other move (the bordered card), and one big arch. The type, the pills, the palette and the copy are identical in all three; only the join changes.

One at a time, so none of them get buried.
