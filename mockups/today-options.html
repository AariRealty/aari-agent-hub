Three ways to dress the mission board

Same three names, same actions, same numbers. What changes is how much the board looks like the rest of the dashboard. In all three the banner and the goal inputs move to the left and the board sits on the right, where you asked for it.

Option 1Ledger

Borrows the row treatment from Team, Deals and Money — hairline rules, a quiet monogram, and actions as underlined text rather than buttons. The lightest of the three, and the one that most disappears into the page.

Option 2Cream cards

Each name in its own cream card with a hairline border, matching the announcement deck exactly. Keeps the pill buttons so the actions stay obvious, and the card edges give each person a bit of weight.

Option 3One at a time

Turns the list into a single call. One person in a cream panel with the actions right there, and everyone else waiting in a compact queue below. Answers who am I calling right now rather than showing three of twenty-two at once.

Which one

I would pick 1, the ledger. The board looked out of place because it was the only card on the page using filled pill buttons and heavy row spacing — the ledger fixes that by reusing a row style the page already has in three other places. 2 is the safer pick if you want the actions to stay loud, since it keeps the buttons and only changes the container. 3 is the most opinionated: it is the best one for actually working the list, and the worst one for seeing the list.
