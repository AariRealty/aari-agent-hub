A plus, and the faces beside it

No Attend button anywhere. The plus is the button. You tap it, it becomes your own circle at the front of the stack, and everyone else who has said yes overlaps along from there. Tap your own circle again to drop out.

All three carry the Google pill from Option U, unchanged: a quiet control in the card header that reads Google: connect now and turns dark and reads Google: on once you have connected. The three differ in where the stack sits and how large it is.

What is real here. Attendance is still not stored anywhere in the database, no table and no column, so all thirteen entries genuinely have nobody on them and every phone below shows a bare plus. Under each phone is the same stack once people start ticking, marked as a shape rather than as data. The agent_google_calendar table exists with zero rows, so the pill honestly reads connect. The entries, times and places are the live feed.

One thing to decide. With the button gone, the only way out of an entry is tapping your own circle. That is a small target, 28px in Option V. Option X uses 32px, which is the comfortable size, and Option W uses 24px, which is the prettiest and the fiddliest.
