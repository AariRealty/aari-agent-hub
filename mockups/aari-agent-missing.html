Which one, and what happens to the three I added

Version A. The document list is the whole point — an agent who sees “0 of 6” still has to go and find out which six. Naming them turns the card from a scold into an instruction, and five of the six are documents she has to go and fetch from somewhere else. B looks better and says less.

Version C is the tidier dashboard and the weaker card. One row saying “Documents, 6” is exactly the “0 of 6” problem again, one level up. Take it if the band is feeling crowded, not because it reads better.

Whichever you pick, I would take Your open file off. It is the fourth copy of a fact you can already see three times, and the documents card carries the same address, so nothing is lost. My listings I would keep — thin today at one property, but it is the only place the address and the zero showings appear, and Milennys has three.
