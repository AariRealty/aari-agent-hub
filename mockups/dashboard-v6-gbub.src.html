The Google bubble, on or off

All three sit at the top right of the Company calendar header, all three read on or off at a glance, and all three open the same sheet when tapped. Number one is what is on the Hub right now.

Why it says off, and why it will keep saying off for now. The state comes from agent_google_calendar, which holds a row for each connected seat and currently holds none. It will only read on once the connection is actually made and recorded, so I have not wired the bubble to flip on tap: that would be showing you a state the Hub does not have.

And why Add to Google did nothing. The published Hub runs inside a preview frame, and that frame blocks a page from opening a new tab. The link fires and the browser drops it, so nothing happens and nothing errors. I have made Copy link the first action in the sheet instead, and added a line saying to paste it into Google Calendar under Other calendars, the plus, From URL. That route works from anywhere. The Add to Google link is still there for when the Hub runs on its own address.
