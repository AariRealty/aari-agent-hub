The calendar, three ways

The explanations under the queue and under Also today are gone, both cards say what they are without a paragraph underneath. The calendar keeps one line of its own for now, each option below handles it differently.

These three are not three grids. One deletes the grid and becomes a list. One keeps dates but only draws the days that have something on them. One leads with the single next thing and folds the rest away.

What is actually wrong with it today.

  • Month view is 818px tall on your phone and 23 of its 31 cells are empty. Five weeks of nothing to look at.
  • Inside the cells the titles are cut to "Liv…", "FR…", "⚡…", so the grid tells you a day is busy but not what with.
  • The week strip draws seven days when only two of them carry anything, and it shows dots, never a name.
  • The title wraps: "CEO Monday LIVE / Call". The attending button and the share circle hold a fixed column, so the name gets what is left.
  • Add to my calendar is the largest control on the card and it is the one you press once, ever. It opens the iCal feed list.
  • There are only 13 entries between Aug 20 and Sep 28, ten of them still ahead of you. A month grid is a lot of furniture for ten things.
