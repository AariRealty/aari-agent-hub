You are right that it is busy. The cover is carrying seven separate pieces: the mark, the town, the day, your name, the number, three lines explaining the number, and the pull bar. On top of that the type is sitting on a lit photograph, so a shade has to be laid over your face to keep the words readable — which is what makes it feel heavy.

All three below are the agent view, the one in your screenshot, so you are comparing like with like. Pull each one up — the hub underneath is the same in all three.
