The template you liked, in our colours

Same bones as the one you sent — a tall hero, a numbered list, an image band, a card at the end, a proper footer. Everything else is ours: Playfair and Poppins instead of Instrument Serif and Inter, the Aari mark at the top, and the palette off the Hub.

The lime had to go. That template is built on #DCFF00, and the house rule is no yellow or gold anywhere. It carried the buttons, the number badges and the whole closing card, so removing it is not a swap — it is the reason the three below differ from each other. Each one answers “what holds the eye now” a different way: white on dark, dark on paper, or taupe.
#DCFF00 lime #D8F90A lime #141210 panel #c9bfa8 taupe #faf5eb warm #fcfcfa paper #1a1a1a ink
Two things I changed on purpose. The original loads four videos off someone else’s CDN — those are their assets, and most inboxes block video anyway, so the image bands are drawn as placeholders for a still. And the original has no real unsubscribe; every footer below carries one, plus the brokerage address, because a letter to your book is a commercial email.
