Where the voice came from. I read the Copy Posse emails in your inbox — hello@copyposse.com, about 201 threads — and wrote up what Alex actually does in docs/alex-cattoni-voice.md. One idea per line. Ellipses between beats. Negate, then correct. The unflattering admission placed in the middle, after the claim, never before it. First name alone as the sign-off. Always a P.S., and it is light. All three letters use that structure; what differs is how each one opens.
Two things in this copy are invented and must not send as they are. The nine-day sale on Bayshore in option A, and the June mispricing in option C, are placeholders — I wrote them to show the shape of the story, not because they happened. Before either goes to ninety-two people the story has to be swapped for a real one out of your own closings. That is exactly the kind of detail that has been read as real before, so I would rather say it loudly than let it slip through.
The separations are the alternating set in all three, so what you are comparing here is the writing, not the shapes. Curves, angles or alternating is still open.

One at a time, so none of them get buried.
