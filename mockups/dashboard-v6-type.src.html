Here they are at the size where the shapes are actually the thing you are looking at. Same words each time. The italic is where the personality lives, so watch that.

Look at three things. The a and the g; how thin the thin strokes go; and how far the italic leans. Those are what separate these, and they are what your logo already has an opinion about.
