Three ways to sound like joinaari.com

The site does two things the Hub does not. It sets its headlines in a high-contrast serif with real ball terminals — the same family feeling as the Aari. mark — and it uses a true italic for the part that matters: Not agents. Aari Business Partners. Its body face is a round, friendly geometric sans, not Helvetica.

Each phone below is exactly the same screen. Only the two faces change, so what you are judging is the type and nothing else. The italic is used the way the site uses it: on the word carrying the meaning.

Also done: the big Marlenyi L. Paredes is off the cover. It sat directly under “Welcome back, Marlenyi”, so the screen said your name twice, the second time in the largest type on it. The welcome now carries it once.
