Somewhere to see every tab, switch role, and choose what shows

Three things are missing on the phone and they belong together. There is no way to see every tab and its sections at once — you only ever see the sections of the tab you are on. The Broker / Agent switch is crammed into the top bar rather than sitting somewhere it can be explained. And What to show is a black bar in the middle of your dashboard, which is not where a setting belongs.

All three options below put the same three things in one place. What differs is how you get there and what shape it takes. Every tab and section listed is the real set.

The cover's mark is already gone. That was a hand-set “Aari REALTY” in Cormorant, not your brand mark — the real one is the image in the bar. The cover now opens straight on the welcome, and the dashboard keeps the proper logo.
