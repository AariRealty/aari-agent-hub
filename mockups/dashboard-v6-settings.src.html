All three, open, so you can see what you are choosing

You did not get to see these, and that is because I hid them. Two of the three had their panel behind a tap, so scrolling past you would have seen three ordinary-looking dashboards and nothing to compare. All three are open below.

Each one carries the same three things: every tab with its sections, the Broker / Agent switch with room to say what the two sides are, and What to show out of the middle of your dashboard. What differs is how you get here and what shape it takes — the chip under each name says which.

The lists are real. Those are the actual tabs and sections of the agent side, including Today now having only one. And they are in the Hub's new type, so this is what it will look like.
