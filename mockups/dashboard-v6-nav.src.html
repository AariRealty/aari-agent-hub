Aari Hub · the bottom of the phone

The bar is two rows tall. Here are three ways to stop that.

What you photographed is two stacked rows — Overview / My day sitting on top of the five sections — plus the strip your phone reserves below them. On your screen that is about 132 pixels, roughly a sixth of the glass, held permanently by controls rather than by your work.

All three keep every section one tap away where they can. Scroll each phone; the third one opens.

The cover is fixed too. The hard line across the top was real and it was mine: the published page carries a 520×700 photograph, shorter than the screen, so its own top edge landed inside the fade. I had been checking a taller copy of the picture where the edge fell almost off the top and barely showed. The photograph now fills the cover whatever shape it is, so there is no edge to see.
