Thursday, six things

All three are drawn on Thursday 27 August, not on today. Thursday is the day everything lands at once: your pop-by day is already saved as Thursday, you want to film then, and the daily conversation and the daily post run every day regardless. If a shape survives Thursday it will survive the rest of the week.

They are also drawn on the true state, which is empty. Nothing is filmed, nothing is logged, and the August edition is unwritten. That is what Thursday actually looks like right now, so it is what you should judge. Under each phone is the same design once a week has run, clearly marked, because an empty card is only half the picture.

Thursday has a quiet piece of good news in it. Your three pop-bys are your three conversations, so the route finishes the day's talking. Thursday is not six jobs, it is four: go out, film, post, write a note. All three options say this, they just say it differently.

The video bank reads zero in all three, and that is real. content_calendar holds no rows. Option C is the only one that treats that as an emergency, which is either the point of it or too much, depending on your taste.

Nothing about the conversations, contacts or pop-by numbers changes here. This is the Today card only. The row above it is a separate decision and still open.

Why it works: it changes nothing you have already signed off. Same rail, same rows, same time column, six entries instead of three. Six rows on a phone is a card you scroll, and the dot is a quiet way to carry a distinction that matters a lot.

Why it works: it is the only one that would have warned you before you missed a day rather than after. The bank is the number you asked for without asking for it: one video a day always is a supply problem, and this is the only shape that treats it as one. Cost: it is the only card mixing a supply you build up with jobs you clear off, so the top row and the bottom two are not really the same kind of number. It is also always a bit alarming, it buries the clock, and it reframes Today as a warning rather than a plan, which is a bigger change than you asked for.

What none of them can do yet. Filming and posting have nowhere to be recorded. content_calendar has the right shape, one dated row per post with a format and a status, but it holds no rows, has no owner column, and has no notion of "every Wednesday and Thursday". Until that exists the video rows are a checkbox that forgets, and the bank cannot be counted at all.

And the same is true of the edition. edition_staging is saved and set to nudge on all eight seats, so the Hub knows how you want to be told. Nothing records that a month's edition went out, so nothing can tick it off or count the days down honestly.
