What changed

  • The counter. A full width black bar becomes the mockup's cream hero, figure first at 52px with the label beneath rather than beside it.
  • The three dropdowns. Still native <select> elements, same events, same handlers. They now wear a pill shell and the Hub's own caret instead of operating system furniture. This is the change I would most like your eye on.
  • Chips, tabs and buttons take the mockup's pill geometry, smaller and tighter.
  • The list rounds to 14px, rows tighten from 18px to 15px padding, and the name goes from 21px to 23px.

Serif sizes were stepped up, not copied

Cormorant carries 27 to 47 per cent less x-height than the Playfair the mockup was drawn in. A straight family swap would have shrunk every serif on the screen. Each one here is sized to match what the mockup looks like rather than what it measures. Tracking on the small Montserrat labels is kept and the rows were given the width instead, per what we agreed.

Two things you should know before you approve

Your item 4 was already built

The list rows on live were already the mockup's design: Cormorant name at weight 500, tier as small tracked uppercase on the right, quiet meta line, hairline dividers, no health colour on the row. There is a comment in the source that says so, crediting an earlier mockup. I proposed building something that existed. What I actually did to the rows was tighten them, not design them.

The header is still tall, and fixing that is a functional change

109px came back, not the 450 the mockup gets to. The mockup's Database screen reaches the first name in about 350px because it has fewer controls. Live carries a score bar, a log button, three tabs, add contact, seven health chips, a search, sort tiers, three filters and a link toggle. Styling cannot remove a control.

The obvious move is to fold Sort tiers and the three filters behind one Filters control, which would take roughly 200px more off. That is behaviour, not appearance, so it is a separate decision and I have not touched it. Say the word and I will mock it.
