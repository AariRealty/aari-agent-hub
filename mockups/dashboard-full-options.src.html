What this buys

  • One column you read instead of scanning four cards for what to do
  • The calendar gets full width, so the week strip stops being cramped
  • Your three keepers sit together as a single block about you

What it costs

  • The doughnut and deal volume get narrower than they are today
  • It is the biggest change of the three — everything moves, even if nothing goes

Which one

Version 2. It changes the three cards that need changing, adds the one thing that is missing, and leaves everything you said you liked exactly where it is. The funnel band costs one row and is the only part of the screen that would have told you, on the 1st of August, that the month was going to end at zero.

Version 1 is the same thing without the funnel — take it if you want the smallest possible change and want to see the three fixed cards on their own first. Version 3 is the one I would build if you find yourself hunting around the screen for what to do next; it is the only version where that question has a single place to look. But it moves your keepers, and you have just told me you like where they are.

The three changed cards are independent of each other. If you want version 2’s layout but a different treatment of the broker card, or the ramp kept full size, say so — none of them is tied to the rest.
