These three are not arrangements. One is a sentence with no grid and no tracks at all. One is a single object rather than four. One deletes the row from the page and puts the figures inside the black card you already have. Every label word survives in all three, at 9px or bigger.

Why the last two sets kept coming out the same. I was treating the row as fixed furniture and only moving it around, because that felt like the safe answer after you told me twice not to remove things. Safe turned into six versions of the same row. Keeping every figure and every word does not require keeping the shape, and these three change the shape.
