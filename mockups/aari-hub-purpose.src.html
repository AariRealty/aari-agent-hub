The Hub tells you what to do today. Everything else is evidence.

You are right that this was never meant to be a CRM. A CRM stores people and waits to be asked. The Hub is meant to open with an instruction and hold you to it. The two sections you pointed at are not instructions, they are evidence, and they have ended up on the one screen that should carry no evidence at all.

Here is the shape as I understand it, where it has drifted, the answer on pop-bys, and three ways to put Today back.

One prospecting plan you own. Each line is an activity, a cadence, the days it lands on, and how many. Today reads it every morning and prints only the lines due. Nothing is hard coded, and adding pop-bys on Fridays is you tapping Friday, not me shipping a change.

What I would do, and in what order

Option A, then the prospecting plan. A is the smallest change that fixes the thing you actually felt, which is that two out of six cards on your morning screen do not tell you to do anything. It also protects everything you have already settled, which C does not.

Then the plan itself, in two steps. First, read pop_by_day so pop-bys appear on Thursday without you doing anything, which is a small change and proves the idea on real data. Then a table for the rest, so activity, cadence, days and count are yours to set for all six lines and the card stops being something only I can move.

The honest cost of A, said plainly: on the morning you want to change your income target, the explanation is no longer on the screen you are looking at, and nothing on Today tells you it moved to Money.
