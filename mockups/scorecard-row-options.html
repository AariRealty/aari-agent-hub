Which one

I would pick A. You have spent the last few rounds making the tabs match each other, and A is the only one that finishes the job — the scorecard row becomes the contact row with a number instead of a button. Nothing new to learn, and the tier badge lands in the same place your eye already looks for it.

C gives the cleanest line under the name — just the call state — but measured at 390px it leaves the name exactly as much room as A does, 199px, and the same one name wraps in both. So the choice between them is taste, not fit. B makes the strongest page on its own and is the only one where the money reads as a ranking, but it wraps one more name and drifts furthest from the other tabs, which is the thing you just said you did not want.

You also asked for the badge on Business. That is worth saying out loud: those tiers are ones I set when the partners became contacts, not something that came out of your database.
