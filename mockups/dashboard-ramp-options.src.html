Which one

Ramp 1. The ramp is a to-do list, and you already have a to-do list on this screen — the one you just approved. Two lists of things to do is how a step gets skipped for four months. Putting the next step in the rail means setup is done in the same place and the same way as everything else, and the dashboard gets no new furniture at all.

Ramp 2 is the one to take if seeing all five at once matters to you while three are outstanding — it is the only version that tells you what is left rather than just what is next. It costs a card until you finish, and then it is gone. Ramp 3 is the quietest of the three and the only one that follows you onto every tab, but a progress bar tucked under the navigation is easy to stop seeing, which is the failure mode you have described more than once.
