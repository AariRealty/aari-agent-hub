The term being waived

Exhibit A §41.1 · Monthly Brokerage Fee
The Monthly Brokerage Fee is set by the Associate's commission plan. Aari Max: $99.00 per month, billed monthly, beginning the month after onboarding.

What this letter does

  1. Aari Realty LLC waives the Monthly Brokerage Fee stated in Exhibit A §41.1 for Marlenyi L. Paredes, in full, from the effective date above until this waiver ends.
  2. Nothing else in the Agreement changes. The commission split, the E&O + Compliance Fee under §41.2, and every other term stand as written.
  3. This waiver is a specific accommodation and is not a course of dealing. It does not waive any other fee, on any other occasion, for this Associate or for any other.
  4. The waiver ends on the condition stated above. It is not perpetual and it does not survive the Associate's separation from the brokerage.

Related party disclosure

Read this part twice

The Associate named in this letter and the broker authorising the waiver are the same person. Marlenyi L. Paredes is the Qualifying Broker of Aari Realty LLC and is also the Associate whose fee is being waived. She is therefore both the party granting this accommodation and the party receiving it.

That is recorded here rather than left to be discovered. The Agreement contains no clause giving the Broker discretion to exempt an Associate from the Brokerage Fee, so this waiver is an act of the Company that sits alongside the Agreement rather than a right exercised under it. Until this letter is signed, the exemption exists only as a boolean on a database row, which is not a record a regulator, an auditor, or a successor broker can read.

Acknowledgement

The Associate acknowledges that the E&O + Compliance Fee under §41.2 is unaffected, that this waiver may be withdrawn in writing at any time, and that on withdrawal the Monthly Brokerage Fee resumes at the rate then stated in §41.1 for the Associate's commission plan.
