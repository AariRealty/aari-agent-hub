What this letter does

  1. Aari Realty LLC waives the Monthly Brokerage Fee stated in Exhibit A §41.1 for Alejandro Paredes, in full, from the effective date above until this waiver ends.
  2. Nothing else in the Agreement changes. The commission split, the E&O + Compliance Fee under §41.2, and every other term stand as written.
  3. This waiver is a specific accommodation and is not a course of dealing. It does not waive any other fee, on any other occasion, for this Associate or for any other.
  4. The waiver ends on the condition stated above. It is not perpetual and it does not survive the Associate's separation from the brokerage.

Related party disclosure

Read this part twice

Alejandro Paredes is the spouse of Marlenyi L. Paredes, the Qualifying Broker of Aari Realty LLC and the person authorising this waiver. The Broker is granting a financial accommodation to a member of her own household.

That relationship is stated here plainly because it is the reason this letter has to exist at all. The Agreement contains no clause giving the Broker discretion to exempt an Associate from the Brokerage Fee, so this waiver is an act of the Company that sits alongside the Agreement rather than a right exercised under it. A related party fee waiver that lives only as a boolean on a database row is precisely the arrangement a review asks about, and the honest answer to that question is a signed letter, not a column.

Before this is signed

Two blanks, deliberately left

The commission plan and the amount are shown as a middle dot rather than a number because the systems disagree. The roster records this seat as 70/30, a legacy split the §41.1 fee table does not contain. The subscription record calls it Aari Max, exempt. A waiver cannot state the amount it waives until the plan of record is settled. Settle the plan first, fill both fields, then sign.
