The Commission Structure Addendum (CSA). It is incorporated into the ICA and governs how commissions are calculated, ordered, offset, and disbursed. Specific fee amounts live in Exhibit A.

Key terms

  • Commission Earned — earned only after the deal closes, funds clear, documents are approved, compliance is met, and the Broker authorizes release.
  • Commission Payable — an earned commission is paid only after compliance review, your obligations are settled, and offsets are applied.
  • Application Order — commissions are applied in a fixed order: compliance holds, referral obligations, Company transaction fees, offsets, then your net share.
  • Offsets & Withholdings — the Company may deduct fees, unpaid balances, referral fees, chargebacks, and fines from your commission; this right survives termination.
  • Plan Application — the plan in effect when the contract is executed (not at closing) governs that deal; plan changes apply prospectively only.
  • Document Hierarchy — the ICA governs the relationship, the CSA governs commission mechanics, and Exhibit A governs plans, splits, and fee amounts.
  • Fixed Splits — referral-sourced and third-party-network deals use fixed splits set in the CSA (Sections 14–16) that override your standard plan.

1. DEFINITIONS

For purposes of this Manual, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

"Active" means an Associate who has completed all onboarding requirements, maintains current license status, has paid all required fees, and is eligible to engage in real estate transactions under this Agreement.

"Inactive" means an Associate who has requested or been placed in inactive status, is not eligible to engage in new real estate transactions, but maintains affiliation with the Company.

"Good Standing" has the meaning set forth exclusively in the INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT Section 7, which is the controlling definition for all purposes under this Manual and all Incorporated Documents.

"Material Breach" includes but is not limited to:

  • (a) Violations resulting in client harm, complaint, or financial loss;
  • (b) Regulatory investigation, fine, or disciplinary action by DBPR, FREC, MLS, or NAR;
  • (c) Repeated violations of the same policy after written warning;
  • (d) Any violation involving fraud, misrepresentation, discrimination, or fair housing law violations;
  • (e) Failure to cure a non-material breach within ten (10) business days of written notice from the Broker.

"Abandoned Commission" means any transaction for which:

  • (a) Closing documents are not submitted within forty-eight (48) hours of closing; or
  • (b) The Associate fails to respond to Broker requests for file completion within ten (10) business days of written request; or
  • (c) For transactions that close after Associate's termination, the Associate fails to submit all closing documents within seven (7) calendar days of the closing date OR within forty-eight (48) hours of receiving actual written notice from the Broker, whichever is later. The closing date per the recorded deed or the Closing Disclosure date shall be conclusive for calculating this deadline. Actual written notice shall be deemed delivered when sent via email to the Associate's last known email address on file with delivery confirmation, or when sent via certified mail, return receipt requested. Associates have an affirmative duty to maintain current contact information and monitor their registered email account. Failure to receive notice due to outdated contact information does not extend deadlines. The Broker shall make reasonable efforts to obtain closing documents directly from title companies and clients for departed Associates.

"Immediately" means within twenty-four (24) hours of the Associate becoming aware of the event, claim, or circumstance requiring notice, excluding weekends and holidays. If the 24-hour period ends on a weekend or holiday, notice must be provided by 9:00 AM EST on the next business day.

"Compliance Issue" means any violation of this Manual, the ICA, or applicable law that does not rise to the level of Material Breach. Associates must cure Compliance Issues within ten (10) business days of the date written notice is sent via email to the Associate's email address on file or posted to the Associate's compliance platform dashboard, whichever occurs first. The date of email transmission or platform posting shall be conclusive as to the start of the cure period.

"Complete File" means a transaction file in which (a) all documents listed in Section 6.1 are submitted, (b) all items on the applicable compliance platform checklist (intake, under-contract, and closing phases) are marked complete, and (c) the file has been reviewed and approved by the Broker.

"Company-Generated Lead" means any prospective client inquiry, contact, or relationship that originated through Company marketing, advertising, lead purchase programs, referral partnerships, or other Company-funded or Company-operated lead generation activities.

"Personal Transaction" means any real estate transaction in which the Associate or the Associate's immediate family member (spouse, parent, child, or sibling) is a principal party (buyer, seller, landlord, or tenant).

"Personally Generated Clients" has the meaning set forth exclusively in the INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT Section 9, which is the controlling definition for all purposes under this Manual and all Incorporated Documents.

"Business Day" means Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST, excluding federal holidays and days when the Company office is closed due to emergency. Emergency office closures will be communicated to all Associates via email and platform notification as soon as practicable. Associates who can demonstrate lack of actual or constructive notice of an emergency closure will receive reasonable deadline extensions.

For time-sensitive matters including wire fraud, E&O claims, and Material Breach notifications, "immediately" means within twenty-four (24) hours calculated on a calendar day basis (not business days), with notice due by the same time on the following day, regardless of weekends or holidays.

Unless otherwise specified, "days" means calendar days.

2. COMMISSIONS & COLLECTIONS

  • The Company may withhold disputed commission amounts and related funds until resolution.
  • Where the Company incurs internal administrative time or outside legal costs to secure or defend a commission, those costs may be allocated proportionally to the Associate. Internal administrative costs shall be calculated at a flat rate of \$150.00 per hour, as may be amended by notice pursuant to EXHIBIT A — COMMISSION FEE SCHEDULE. In transactions where the Associate receives ninety percent (90%) or more of the gross commission, cost allocation shall be capped at eighty percent (80%) of total costs incurred, with the Company absorbing the remaining costs as part of its supervisory obligations. For all other transactions, proportional allocation shall be calculated as the ratio of the Associate's commission split to the total commission earned by the Company on the transaction. In transactions where commission is reduced, reversed, or uncollectible, allocation is based on the originally contracted commission amount. In transactions involving multiple Associates, allocation is divided pro-rata based on each Associate's split percentage. The Broker will provide written accounting of all allocated costs, including itemized breakdown of internal administrative time and external legal expenses, within ten (10) business days of deduction.
  • The Company is not obligated to prosecute or sue to collect any commission, and Associates have no right to compel the Company to initiate collection or legal action. If the Company elects to pursue collection or legal action, reasonable costs and attorneys' fees may be deducted proportionally from recovered amounts.
  • Associates must complete all compliance requirements within the timeframes specified in this Manual; commissions may be held until files are complete and approved.

FGCMLS Remarks Rules — what you can and can't write
Public Remarks vs. Confidential Remarks. Violations result in fines.

The MLS distinguishes between Public Remarks (visible to the public on consumer sites like Zillow) and Confidential Remarks (visible only to MLS subscribers). The rules for each are different. Misuse triggers fines from FGCMLS.

Public Remarks — Allowed

  • Property features, layout, finishes, recent updates
  • Neighborhood characteristics (schools, parks, lifestyle)
  • HOA / community amenities
  • Property condition descriptions consistent with the listing

Public Remarks — NOT Allowed

  • Agent name, brokerage name, or contact information
  • Showing instructions or access details (lockbox codes, alarm codes, gate codes)
  • Commission information or co-broke offers
  • Open house dates and times (use the dedicated open house field)
  • URLs, phone numbers, or email addresses
  • Discriminatory language under Fair Housing
  • Speculative or unverifiable claims ("best deal in town," "must see")

Confidential Remarks — Allowed

  • Showing instructions and lockbox details
  • Alarm codes and gate codes
  • Agent-to-agent notes (offer instructions, deadline reminders)
  • Co-broke commission terms
  • Owner-occupied vs. tenant-occupied notes
  • Pet warnings or other access cautions

Confidential Remarks — NOT Allowed

  • Discriminatory language under Fair Housing (applies to all remarks)
  • Information that should appear in the listing's structured fields

REALTOR® Trademark
Don't use the word "realtor" casually
"REALTOR®" is a registered trademark of the National Association of REALTORS®. Misuse creates legal exposure for the agent and the brokerage.
  • Use only if — You are an active member in good standing of NAR and your local Realtor association.
  • Format — Always all caps with the registered symbol: REALTOR®. Never lowercase, never without the ®.
  • If not a NAR member — Use "Real Estate Agent," "Sales Associate," or "Licensed in Florida." Do not use "realtor" in any form.
  • Marketing materials — Bios, business cards, signs, social media, and email signatures must follow this rule.
  • Authority — NAR Code of Ethics; ICA §3 (Associate Responsibilities); Advertising Policy.
