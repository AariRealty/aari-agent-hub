How choosing works

Three steps, and you can change your mind.

01

Pick a name

Read the cards and choose whoever you’d rather talk to. If nobody jumps out, send it to the brokerage and Marlenyi will match you herself.

02

They reach out

Your message goes to that agent with their name already on the form. Usually the same day, always from a real person — not an autoresponder.

03

You decide to continue

A first conversation is not a commitment. If it isn’t a fit, say so and we’ll switch you without any awkwardness. That’s the whole point of letting you choose.

Read this before you choose

Choose an agent for any reason that matters to you — how they explain things, the language you’d rather speak, availability, whatever it is.
