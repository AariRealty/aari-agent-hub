What you actually get

Six things that change how you work.

01

A broker who answers

Direct access to the broker-owner. Real conversations about real deals, in real time. No ticketing system, no waiting three days for a compliance answer while your inspection window closes.

02

The Mentorship plan

A plan where the split pays for working directly with Marlenyi on the deal. Your listings, your buyers, your objections, on the file you are actually working.

03

No franchise, royalty or desk fees

No franchise fees. No royalty fees. No desk fees. What you keep is set out in writing before you sign, with nothing skimmed off the top on the way through.

04

Transaction coordination covered

Your files run through Aari Transactions. Deadlines tracked, documents complete, closing on time. Less paperwork means more time in front of clients.

05

E&O covered

Errors and omissions coverage is carried on your files, so you are not arranging it deal by deal or wondering whether you are covered mid transaction.

06

English and Spanish

The brokerage runs in both languages. Your clients get documents explained and questions answered in the language they are most comfortable in, and so do you.
