The reality

You're not underperforming. You're unsupported.

Most agents who leave the business don't leave because they couldn't sell. They leave because nobody ever built them a system. Any of this sound familiar?

  • You're at a large brokerage and your broker doesn't know your name.
  • You're drowning in paperwork nobody ever taught you how to run.
  • You closed deals last year but you still have no repeatable process.
  • The "training" is a recorded webinar and a Facebook group.
  • You're paying a split for a logo, not for support.

None of that is a character flaw. It's an operations gap, and it's fixable.

What you actually get

Six things that change how you work.

Access

A broker who answers

Direct access to the broker-owner. Real conversations about real deals, in real time. No ticketing system, no waiting three days for a compliance answer while your inspection window closes.

Mentorship

One-on-one, not one-to-many

Individual mentorship with someone invested in your business. We work your actual pipeline — your listings, your buyers, your objections — not a generic curriculum recorded three years ago.

Coaching

Weekly coaching that's current

Strategy sessions built on what is happening in this market right now: how inventory is moving, what's actually getting offers accepted, and where the deals are coming from this quarter.

Operations

Transaction coordination covered

Your files run through Aari Transactions. Deadlines tracked, documents complete, closing on time. Less paperwork on your desk means more time in front of clients — which is the only thing that grows a business.

Tools

CRM and systems included

A CRM that keeps your database and follow-up on track, plus the templates, checklists and workflows that turn scattered effort into a repeatable process.

Growth

Marketing and branding support

Listing marketing, content and branding help so your business looks like a business. Support with your presence, not just the brokerage's.

On compensation

Splits are graduated — they increase as your production increases — with the terms stated plainly in your independent contractor agreement before you ever sign it. No buried fees, no fine print you find out about at your first closing. Ask and we'll walk through the full schedule in the first conversation.

Fit matters more than headcount

We'd rather say no than say yes to the wrong person.

This isn't for you if

  • You want a place to park your license and be left alone.
  • You treat compliance as paperwork to get around.
  • You want leads handed to you with no business of your own.
  • You're shopping purely on split and nothing else.
  • You're not willing to be coached on how you're currently working.

This is for you if

  • You want structure, systems and someone who will tell you the truth.
  • You take compliance seriously because it protects your clients and your license.
  • You're building a business, not chasing one transaction at a time.
  • You want to be a bigger part of a smaller operation.
  • You're new and want real mentorship, or experienced and tired of being a number.
