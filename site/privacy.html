Privacy policy

This policy explains how Aari Realty LLC ("Aari Realty," "we," "us") collects, uses and protects information you give us through this website. It applies to aarirealty.com and the forms on it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us directly: your name, email address, phone number, property address, and anything you write in a message or form field.
  • Information about how you use the site: pages visited, referring site, approximate location by IP address, browser and device type — collected in aggregate through standard web analytics.
  • Information you provide during a transaction, if you become a client. That is governed by your representation agreement and by Florida real estate law, not only by this policy.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide the real estate services you asked about.
  • To send you information you requested — a market analysis, listing alerts, an area comparison.
  • To meet our record-keeping obligations as a licensed Florida real estate brokerage.
  • To improve the site and understand which content is actually useful.

How we contact you

When you submit a form, you agree that we may contact you by phone, text message or email about your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, and out of marketing email using the unsubscribe link. We will still be able to reach you about an active transaction you are a party to.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where it is necessary to do the work:

  • Service providers who operate the site and our systems on our behalf — our form and email providers, our CRM — under agreements that limit their use of it.
  • Parties to your transaction, with your knowledge: a title company, lender, inspector, or the other side’s brokerage.
  • Affiliated Aari entities where you asked for that service, such as Aari Transactions for coordination or Aari Referrals for a referral.
  • Regulators, auditors or courts where the law requires it, including the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses cookies and similar technologies for basic functionality and aggregate analytics. Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies; parts of the site may not work as well if you do. We do not use this site to build advertising profiles about individuals.

How long we keep it

Inquiries that do not become transactions are kept only as long as they are useful for following up with you. Transaction records are retained for the period required by Florida law and by our brokerage record-keeping policy, which is longer.

Your choices

  • Ask us what information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete information, where we are not required to retain it by law.
  • Opt out of marketing messages at any time.

Email hello@aarirealty.com or call 239.688.1770 and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information we hold. No website or email system is perfectly secure. Never send wire instructions, bank account numbers, Social Security numbers or other sensitive financial details through a web form or unencrypted email. Wire fraud in real estate is real: always verify wire instructions by calling your title company at a number you already have, never a number sent to you in an email.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be noted here.

Terms of use

By using this site you agree to these terms. If you don’t agree, please don’t use the site.

Information is general, not advice

Everything on this site — the buyer guide, the seller guide, the relocation guide, the tables and the FAQs — is general education about how real estate transactions typically work in Southwest Florida. It is not legal, tax, accounting, lending, insurance or investment advice, and it is not a substitute for advice about your specific situation from a qualified professional. Program rules, costs, customs and market conditions change.

No guarantee of results

Nothing on this site is a promise or guarantee of a sale, a purchase, a price, a timeline, an income level or any other outcome. Market conditions, property condition, financing and your own decisions all affect results.

Commissions are negotiable

All real estate brokerage commissions and fees are fully negotiable between you and the brokerage. They are not set by law, by any real estate association, or by any multiple listing service. The compensation that applies to you is the compensation stated in the written agreement you sign.

Property information

Property information displayed on or linked from this site is believed to be accurate but is not warranted. Listings may be sold, withdrawn or changed without notice. Square footage, lot dimensions, flood zone, taxes, HOA and CDD figures and permit history should be independently verified by you and your professionals before you rely on them.

Third-party links

We link to other sites, including our affiliated Aari businesses and outside resources. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of sites we do not operate.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Florida, and any dispute arising from the use of this site will be brought in the state or federal courts serving Lee County, Florida.

Accessibility

Aari Realty is committed to making this site usable by everyone, including people using screen readers, keyboard navigation or other assistive technology. We build to widely accepted accessibility guidelines and we test the site as it changes.

If any part of this site is a barrier for you, tell us and we will fix it — and in the meantime we will give you the same information another way. Email hello@aarirealty.com or call 239.688.1770.

Fair housing

Aari Realty LLC is an Equal Housing Opportunity brokerage. We fully support and comply with the federal Fair Housing Act, the Florida Fair Housing Act, and all applicable state and local fair housing laws.

We do not discriminate in the sale, rental, financing or advertising of housing, or in the provision of real estate brokerage services, on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, familial status, national origin, disability, or any other characteristic protected under federal, state or local law.
