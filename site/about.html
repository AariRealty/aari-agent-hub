About · Aari Realty LLC

A small brokerage on purpose.

Built by an operator, not a salesperson. The goal was never to be the biggest office in Lee County — it was a brokerage where clients are actually informed and every file could survive an audit on any given day.

The story

It started with a bad experience and a better idea.

Marlenyi Paredes bought her first home before she ever sold one. That purchase changed her family’s trajectory — and it also showed her how much of this process runs on assumed knowledge. Documents arrive without explanation. Deadlines pass without warning. You’re expected to already understand a system nobody has walked you through.

She had spent years before that building and running businesses with her husband. That background made the gap obvious: this wasn’t a knowledge problem, it was an operations problem. Nobody had built the system.

So she got licensed, then went further — earning a broker’s license rather than stopping at a sales license, specifically so she could set the standard rather than work under someone else’s.

Today Aari is a working brokerage in Lehigh Acres serving buyers, sellers and agents across Lee and Collier County — still deliberately small, still built the same way.

The Aari Standard

Six commitments. Every file, every time.

Not values on a wall. The operating rules the brokerage runs on, and what you can hold any Aari agent to.

01

Explain before you ask for a signature

Nobody here sends a document without walking through what it does. If the client doesn’t understand it, the agent isn’t finished.

02

Every file is audit-ready

Disclosures complete, deadlines documented, signatures in order, communication logged. That’s what protects the client and the agent when something goes sideways.

03

Say the hard thing early

Overpriced listings, unrealistic budgets, properties with problems. Being agreeable in month one and honest in month four costs the client money.

04

Fair housing is not negotiable

We do not steer, we do not discriminate, and we do not describe people when we’re describing property. No exceptions.

05

The broker is reachable

Clients and agents get a broker who answers, not a ticketing system. Only possible because we stay deliberately small — which is the point.

06

Systems, not heroics

Good outcomes shouldn’t depend on someone remembering. Coordination, checklists and tracked deadlines hold the standard on the busiest week of the year.
