01

What is this move actually fixing?

Name it before you name a price. Every trade-off later gets easier.

  • Three things you’d walk away over
  • What you’d pay a little more for
  • The date that isn’t moving
02

Your real monthly number, built first.

Full payment estimate before you tour anything. Then we work backwards to a price range.

  • Taxes at the rate that applies after the sale
  • Insurance, and flood where it applies
  • The seller’s homestead does not transfer to you
03

Pre-approved. Not pre-qualified.

Pre-qualification is a conversation. Pre-approval is a letter a listing agent takes seriously.

  • Talk to two or three lenders
  • Compare the full cost, not the phone rate
  • Then: no new credit, no job changes

Aari Realty is not a lender and makes no lending decisions or recommendations. You choose your lender; we receive nothing for an introduction.

04

Pick your agent, put it in writing.

Florida requires a written buyer agreement before touring. That’s a good thing — it says what you’re owed.

  • What your agent owes you, in writing
  • How long it runs
  • How they’re paid, and by whom

Commissions are fully negotiable and are not set by law, by any association, or by any MLS. We walk through ours line by line first.

05

Shop with a filter, not a feed.

A direct MLS feed matched to your criteria, so you see it when it happens — not days later.

  • Roof, HVAC and water heater age
  • Permit history on additions and lanais
  • Flood zone, elevation, how it drains
06

Write it strong, hold your line.

An offer is more than a number. You get a written cash-to-close estimate before you sign.

  • Deposit — how much, and when it’s at risk
  • Inspection window — days to inspect or walk
  • What conveys, and the closing date
07

The stretch where deals quietly die.

Every date calendared from the day the contract is executed. This is what our coordination exists for.

  • Inspection, wind mitigation, four-point
  • Appraisal — and your options if it’s low
  • Title, survey, estoppel, insurance bound
08

Walkthrough, closing, keys.

You see your figures before closing day, not at the table.

  • Call the title company to verify wire instructions — never trust email
  • Utilities set to transfer
  • File homestead if it’s your primary

Financing

Four loan paths, one at a time.

General education only. Confirm with a licensed lender.

01Conventional
02FHA
03VA
04USDA

Conventional

Down payment
As little as 3% for qualifying buyers
Often fits
Stronger credit, steadier documented income
Worth knowing
Mortgage insurance typically applies under 20% down — and can usually come off later as equity builds

FHA

Down payment
3.5% with qualifying credit
Often fits
First-time buyers, or buyers rebuilding credit
Worth knowing
Friendlier credit standards, stricter property condition rules, and mortgage insurance often for the life of the loan

VA

Down payment
None for eligible service members and veterans
Often fits
Active duty, veterans, some surviving spouses
Worth knowing
No monthly mortgage insurance. A funding fee usually applies

USDA Rural Development

Down payment
None in eligible areas
Often fits
Buyers inside the income limits, in an eligible location
Worth knowing
Both the address and your household income have to qualify
