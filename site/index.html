Where we work

Southwest Florida, market by market.

Different budgets buy very different things across Lee and Collier County. Here's the honest version of each.

Lehigh Acres

Our home base. The most house per dollar in Lee County, heavy new construction, wide variation block to block. Lot, flood zone and road access matter more here than anywhere else.

Fort Myers

The widest range in the region — established neighborhoods, gated communities, downtown condos. Insurance and flood exposure vary sharply by corridor.

Cape Coral

Canal and Gulf-access waterfront plus a large inland new-build market. Assessments, city utilities and seawall condition decide value.

Estero & Bonita Springs

Amenity communities and golf between Fort Myers and Naples. You're trading square footage for location — run the HOA and CDD math first.

Naples

Collier County pricing, standards and negotiation culture. A different market than Lee. We work it, and we'll tell you plainly when it isn't yours.

North Fort Myers & Alva

Larger lots, acreage and manufactured-home communities. Well and septic, zoning and permitted use decide whether a deal works.

Questions

The ones we get every week.

How much do I need saved to buy here?
Less than most people assume, and more than the down payment alone. Plan for the down payment plus closing costs, prepaid taxes and insurance, and inspections. Several loan programs allow 0% to 3.5% down. We'll build a written estimate for your situation before you tour anything.
What's my home worth right now?
An online estimate isn't a price. We send a comparative market analysis — real closed comps adjusted for condition, what's competing with you, what failed to sell and why, and a net sheet at three price points. No cost and no obligation to list with us.
Do I really need flood insurance?
It depends on the property's flood zone and your lender. In parts of Lee and Collier it's required; elsewhere it's optional but often still worth carrying. We check zone and elevation on every home you seriously consider, before you write an offer, because it changes your monthly payment.
How are commissions handled?
All real estate commissions are fully negotiable and are not set by law, by any association, or by any MLS. Whatever applies to you is agreed in writing before you tour homes or sign a listing agreement, and we walk through it line by line first.
Do you work with first-time buyers?
Most of our clients are. First-time buyers, growing families, and renters moving into ownership in the 50K–00K range are exactly who this brokerage was built for. Expect more explanation, not less.
I'm an agent. Can I hang my license here?
Yes — carefully and not constantly. One-on-one mentorship, weekly coaching, transaction coordination covered, CRM and marketing support, and splits that increase with production. See what joining looks like.
