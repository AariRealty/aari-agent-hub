Questions

The stuff you’re actually wondering.

How much do I need saved to buy here?+
More than the down payment alone. Plan for the down payment, closing costs, prepaid taxes and insurance, and inspections. What you actually need depends on the loan you qualify for, and only a licensed loan officer can tell you that — talk to two or three of your own choosing. Bring us what they send you and we will make sure you understand every line before you tour.
What is my home actually worth?+
An online estimate is not a price. We send a comparative market analysis: real closed comps adjusted for condition, what is competing with you right now, what failed to sell and why, and an estimated net sheet at three price points, so you are looking at what you would take home rather than a list price on its own. No cost, and no requirement to list with us. An analysis is an opinion of value — not an appraisal, and not a guarantee of sale price.
How do commissions work?+
All real estate commissions are fully negotiable and are not set by law, by any association, or by any multiple listing service. Whatever applies to you is agreed in writing before you tour homes or sign a listing agreement, and we walk through it line by line first.
Do I really need flood insurance?+
It depends on the property flood zone and on what your lender requires. In parts of Lee and Collier County it is required; elsewhere it is optional. We check zone and elevation on every home you seriously consider, before you write an offer. Your insurance agent quotes what the coverage costs, and that quote belongs in your monthly number before you commit.
Why will my taxes not match the seller’s?+
Florida caps annual assessment increases for existing homesteaded owners, and those savings do not transfer to you. The property is typically reassessed after the sale, so the seller current bill is not a forecast of yours. Budget from the reassessed figure rather than the seller’s current bill, and confirm it with the county property appraiser. If the home becomes your primary residence, you file for homestead exemption yourself.
What is the difference between HOA and CDD?+
An HOA fee covers community operations. A CDD assessment repays the infrastructure bonds that built the community and often appears on your tax bill, not your HOA statement. Buyers regularly miss the second one entirely. Both belong in your monthly number.
Can I list without full service?+
Yes. There are two service levels. Full service means we run and negotiate the whole thing. Listing only gets your home into the MLS and syndicated, and you handle showings, offers and negotiation. We will tell you which fits, including when the answer is the lighter one. Either way the fee is negotiated with you and put in writing before you sign.
What about new construction?+
The on-site sales representative works for the builder. You can still have your own agent represent you, but you generally need to bring that agent on your first visit. How your agent is compensated is negotiable and agreed with you in writing before you tour. Come to us before you register, and we will walk through the builder contract with you, which is written to protect the builder, and we recommend a Florida real estate attorney review it before you sign.
I am an agent. Can I hang my license here?+
Yes. Direct access to the broker, Aari Transactions handling the paperwork on your files, E&O covered, service in English and Spanish, and no franchise, royalty or desk fees. There is also a Mentorship plan, where the split pays for working directly with Marlenyi on the deal. See what joining looks like.
