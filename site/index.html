Common questions

Questions clients ask before their first showing.

How much do I need saved to buy here?
Less than most people assume, and more than the down payment alone. Plan for the down payment plus closing costs, prepaid taxes and insurance, and inspections. Several programs allow 0% to 3.5% down. We build a written estimate for your situation before you tour anything.
What is my home actually worth?
An online estimate is not a price. We send a comparative market analysis: real closed comps adjusted for condition, what’s competing with you right now, what failed to sell and why, and a net sheet at three price points. No cost, and no requirement to list with us.
How do commissions work?
All real estate commissions are fully negotiable and are not set by law, by any association, or by any multiple listing service. Whatever applies to you is agreed in writing before you tour homes or sign a listing agreement, and we walk through it line by line first.
Do I really need flood insurance?
It depends on the property’s flood zone and your lender’s requirements. In parts of Lee and Collier County it is required; elsewhere it’s optional but often still worth carrying. We check zone and elevation on every home you seriously consider, before you write an offer — it changes your monthly payment.
Why won’t my taxes match the seller’s?
Florida caps annual assessment increases for existing homesteaded owners, and those savings don’t transfer to you. The property is typically reassessed after the sale, so the seller’s current bill is not a forecast of yours. We budget from the reassessed figure, then you file for homestead exemption if it’s your primary residence.
What’s the difference between HOA and CDD?
An HOA fee covers community operations. A CDD assessment repays the infrastructure bonds that built the community and often appears on your tax bill, not your HOA statement. Buyers regularly miss the second one entirely. Both belong in your monthly number.
Do I have to use full service?
No. We offer two service levels. Full service means we run and negotiate the whole thing. Listing only gets your home into the MLS and syndicated, and you handle showings, offers and negotiation. We’ll tell you which fits — including when the answer is the lighter one. Either way the fee is negotiated with you and put in writing before you sign.
What about new construction?
The on-site sales representative works for the builder. You can still have your own agent represent you — in most cases at no additional cost to you — but you generally need to bring that agent on your first visit. Come to us before you register, and we’ll review the builder’s contract, which is written to protect the builder.
I’m an agent. Can I hang my license here?
Yes — carefully, not constantly. One-on-one mentorship, weekly coaching, transaction coordination covered on your files, CRM and marketing support, and splits that increase with production. See what joining looks like.
