Why Aari exists

Most people don't lose money on a bad house.
They lose it on a bad process.

Marlenyi Paredes bought her first home before she ever sold one. She remembers being handed documents she didn't understand and being told to sign quickly. Aari Realty was built so that never happens to the people who hire us — and so the agents who work here have the systems to make sure it doesn't.

  • A DocuSign link with no explanation of what you're signing.

What we believe

The four things we don't bend on.

Clarity beats pressure.

If a decision only makes sense when you're rushed, it's not a good decision. We'd rather you take another week and be certain than close fast and regret it. Nobody at Aari is compensated for talking you into something.

A clean file protects you.

Disclosures, addenda, deadlines and signatures handled correctly aren't paperwork — they're what stands between you and a problem two years from now. We run audit-ready files because that's what protects clients.

Local has to mean local.

Flood zones, assessments, insurance carriers, HOA rules, which Lehigh Acres blocks appraise and which don't. That knowledge doesn't transfer from another market. It's earned here, and we work here.

The broker should be reachable.

You get the broker-owner's attention, not a call center. Small brokerage on purpose: fewer clients at once, higher standard on each one, and a person who is accountable for the outcome.

Where we work

Southwest Florida, market by market.

Different budgets buy very different things across Lee and Collier County. Here is the honest version of each.

Lehigh Acres

Our home base. The most house per dollar in Lee County, heavy new construction, wide variation block to block. Lot, flood zone and road access matter more here than anywhere else.

Fort Myers

The widest range in the region — established neighborhoods, gated communities, downtown condos. Insurance and flood exposure vary sharply by corridor.

Cape Coral

Canal and Gulf-access waterfront, plus a large inland new-construction market. Assessments, city utilities and seawall condition are the details that decide value.

Estero & Bonita Springs

Between Fort Myers and Naples. Strong amenity communities and golf. Buyers here are usually trading location for HOA and CDD costs — worth running the math.

Naples

Collier County pricing and standards. A different negotiation culture and a different inventory pattern than Lee. We work it, and we tell you plainly when it isn't your market.

North Fort Myers & Alva

Larger lots, acreage and manufactured-home communities. Well and septic, zoning, and land use are the questions that decide whether a deal works.
