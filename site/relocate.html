What nobody tells you

Six things that work differently than where you're coming from.

Insurance is a real line item

Homeowners and windstorm coverage here can be a significant part of your monthly payment, and it depends heavily on roof age and construction type. Get a quote on the specific property before you're emotionally committed to it.

Flood zone is property-specific

Two homes on the same street can sit in different flood zones with very different requirements and costs. Elevation certificates matter. We check zone and elevation on every home you seriously consider.

HOA and CDD are two different things

An HOA fee covers community operations. A CDD assessment repays infrastructure bonds and often appears on your tax bill, not your HOA statement. Buyers regularly miss the second one entirely.

Your taxes won't match the seller's

Florida caps annual assessment increases for existing homesteaded owners, and those savings don't transfer. The property is typically reassessed after the sale — budget from that figure, then file for homestead exemption.

Hurricane season is a plan, not a fear

June through November. It means shutters or impact windows, a roof you know the age of, a policy you've actually read, and closing timelines that account for insurance binding restrictions when a storm is named.

No state income tax changes the math

For many relocating buyers it offsets a meaningful part of the insurance and tax picture. Whether it nets out in your favor depends on your situation — that's a question for your CPA, and we'll say so rather than pretend to answer it.

Buying from a distance

How it works when you can't fly in every weekend.

We do this regularly. It works when the process is disciplined.

1

Define the real brief

Budget, timeline, commute or airport needs, schools, community or privacy, and whether this is primary, seasonal or investment. That conversation eliminates most of the region and saves you months.

2

Area before property

We narrow to two or three markets and explain the trade-offs honestly, including where your budget goes further and where it doesn't. Choosing the area first makes every property decision easier.

3

Video tours that show what photos hide

Live walkthroughs where you direct the camera, plus recordings to re-watch with family. We film the road, the neighbors, the drainage, the roof, the panel and the water heater.

4

Offer, inspect, verify remotely

Your estimated cash to close in writing before signing. We attend the inspection on your behalf and send video of anything that matters. Remote online notarization and e-signature available for most transactions.

One visit, timed to matter

If you're only coming once, come when it counts — to see the shortlist in person, or for the final walkthrough. We'll tell you which visit is worth the flight for your situation instead of asking you to make several.
