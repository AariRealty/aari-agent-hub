What nobody tells you

Six things that work differently than where you're coming from.

Insurance is a real line item.

Homeowners and windstorm coverage in this region can be a significant part of your monthly payment, and the amount depends heavily on the roof's age and construction type. Get a quote on the specific property before you're emotionally committed to it, not after.

Flood zone is property-specific.

Two homes on the same street can sit in different flood zones and carry very different requirements and costs. Elevation certificates matter. We check zone and elevation on every home you seriously consider, before an offer.

HOA and CDD are two different things.

An HOA fee covers community operations. A CDD assessment repays the infrastructure bonds that built the community and often appears on your tax bill, not your HOA statement. Buyers regularly miss the second one entirely. Both belong in your monthly number.

Your taxes won't match the seller's.

Florida caps annual assessment increases for existing homesteaded owners, and those savings don't transfer to you. The property is typically reassessed after the sale, so budget from the reassessed figure. Then file for homestead exemption if it's your primary residence.

Hurricane season is a plan, not a fear.

It runs June through November. It means shutters or impact windows, a roof you know the age of, an insurance policy you've actually read, and closing timelines that account for insurance binding restrictions when a storm is named. Locals plan for it. So should you.

No state income tax changes the math.

Florida has no state income tax, which for many relocating buyers offsets a meaningful part of the insurance and tax picture. Whether it nets out in your favor depends on your situation — that's a question for your CPA, and we'll say so rather than pretend to answer it.

Buying from a distance

How it works when you can't fly in every weekend.

We do this regularly. It works when the process is disciplined.

01

A call to define the actual brief.

Budget, timeline, commute or airport needs, schools, whether you want a community or privacy, and whether this is a primary home, a seasonal home or an investment. That conversation eliminates most of the region and saves you months.

02

Area orientation before property search.

We narrow to two or three markets and explain the trade-offs honestly, including where your budget goes further and where it doesn't. Choosing the area first makes every property decision afterward far easier.

03

Video tours that show the parts photos hide.

Live walkthroughs where you direct the camera, plus recorded walkthroughs you can re-watch with your family. We film the road, the neighbors, the drainage, the roof, the electrical panel and the water heater — the things listing photos are framed to avoid.

04

Offer, inspect, and verify remotely.

You'll have your estimated cash to close in writing before signing. We attend the inspection on your behalf and send video of anything that matters. Remote online notarization and electronic signature are available for most transactions.

05

One visit, timed to matter.

If you're only coming once, come when it counts: to see the shortlist in person, or for the final walkthrough. We'll tell you which visit is worth the flight for your situation instead of asking you to make several.
