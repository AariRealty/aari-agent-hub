What nobody tells you

Six things that work differently than where you’re coming from.

01

Insurance is a real line item

Homeowners and windstorm coverage here can be a significant part of your monthly payment, and it depends heavily on roof age and construction type. Get a quote on the specific property before you’re emotionally committed to it.

02

Flood zone is property-specific

Two homes on the same street can sit in different flood zones with very different requirements and costs. We check zone and elevation on every home you seriously consider.

03

HOA and CDD are two different things

An HOA fee covers community operations. A CDD assessment repays infrastructure bonds and often appears on your tax bill, not your HOA statement. Buyers regularly miss the second one entirely.

04

Your taxes won’t match the seller’s

Florida caps annual assessment increases for existing homesteaded owners, and those savings don’t transfer. The property is typically reassessed after the sale — budget from that figure, then file for homestead exemption.

05

Hurricane season is a plan, not a fear

June through November. Shutters or impact windows, a roof you know the age of, a policy you’ve read, and closing timelines that account for insurance binding restrictions when a storm is named.

06

No state income tax changes the math

For many relocating buyers it offsets a meaningful part of the insurance and tax picture. Whether it nets out in your favor is a question for your CPA, and we’ll say so rather than pretend to answer it.
