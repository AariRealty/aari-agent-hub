The plan

You get one launch. Six moves.

Tap a move. It advances on its own.

01

Strategy and net sheet.

We walk the home and give you an estimated net at three price points — so the conversation is what you take home, not a list price in a vacuum.

  • Price, timing and showing instructions, agreed in writing
  • How compensation works, before you sign

Commissions and fees are fully negotiable and are not set by law, by any association, or by any MLS.

02

Prepare. Not renovate.

You need the handful of items that move perceived value. We’ll tell you which are worth your money and which to skip.

  • Declutter the rooms buyers photograph in their heads
  • Fix what an inspection would surface and cost you leverage
  • Permits, roof and HVAC ages, wind mitigation, HOA — ready up front
03

Launch everywhere at once.

A new listing gets its biggest audience in its first two weeks. We spend all of it on day one.

  • Professional photography
  • MLS copy written to be searched and read
  • Portal syndication and a social push, same window
04

Showings, and honest adjustment.

A weekly update with the actual numbers — showings, views, saves, and what buyers’ agents said.

  • If something needs to change, you hear it in week two
  • Not week eight
05

Price is one term of several.

Every offer evaluated on the full picture and presented to you plainly.

  • Financing type and how strong the pre-approval is
  • Deposit, and how fast it becomes non-refundable
  • Inspection and financing windows — your exposure
  • Appraisal terms if it comes in low
06

Contract to close, tracked.

A dedicated transaction coordinator runs the file to the wire.

  • Every deadline calendared
  • Repairs, appraisal and title coordinated
  • Your closing figures reviewed before closing day

The money

What comes out before you get paid.

The categories that appear on a Florida closing statement, so nothing on yours is a surprise.

01Payoff & fees
02Taxes & title
03Property items

Payoff and fees

Mortgage payoff
Principal plus interest through the payoff date
Brokerage compensation
Fully negotiable, agreed in writing in your listing agreement

Taxes and title

Documentary stamp tax
Customarily the seller. A state transfer tax on the sale price
Title policy & settlement
Varies by county and by contract — local custom differs between Lee and Collier
Property taxes
Prorated to the closing date and credited to the buyer

Property items

HOA or condo estoppel
Required where an association applies. Dues prorated
Negotiated repairs
Completed before closing, or credited at closing
Liens & open permits
Must be resolved before title will clear

General information only. Every term above is negotiable in your contract. Aari Realty does not provide legal, tax or accounting advice.
