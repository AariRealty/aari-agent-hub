Step one

An online estimate is not a price.

Automated valuations don’t know you replaced the roof in 2023, that the house behind you backs to a preserve, or that two of the comps they used were distressed sales.

What we send is a comparative market analysis: real closed comps adjusted for condition, what’s competing with you right now, what failed to sell and why, current absorption in your neighborhood, and a recommended list price with the reasoning in writing.

No cost, no obligation

No requirement to list with us. If your best move is to wait a season or fix two things first, that’s what the analysis will say.

Street, city and zip is enough.

Pricing strategy

You get one launch. The first fourteen days decide the rest.

A new listing gets its largest audience in its first two weeks. Price it correctly and you compete for the strongest buyers. Price it high to “leave room” and you spend that attention teaching the market that your home is overpriced.

01

Strategy and net sheet

We walk the home, build the analysis, and give you an estimated net sheet at three price points — so the conversation is about what you take home, not a list price in isolation. Then we agree in writing on price, timing, showing instructions and how compensation works.

02

Prepare the home

You don’t need a renovation. You need the handful of items that actually move perceived value, and we’ll tell you which are worth your money and which to skip.

  • Declutter the rooms buyers photograph in their heads
  • Repairs that would surface in an inspection and cost you leverage
  • Curb appeal — the first photo and the first impression are the same thing
  • Documents ready up front: permits, roof and HVAC ages, wind mitigation, HOA
03

Launch everywhere at once

Professional photography, the MLS listing written to be searched and read, syndication to the major consumer portals, and a coordinated social push timed to the same window.

04

Showings and honest adjustment

A weekly update with the numbers: showings, online views, saves, and what buyers and their agents actually said. If the data says something needs to change, we tell you in week two, not week eight.

05

Offers and negotiation

Price is one term of several. We evaluate every offer on the full picture and present it plainly.

  • Financing type and strength of the pre-approval
  • Deposit amount and how quickly it becomes non-refundable
  • Inspection and financing windows — how long you’re exposed
  • Appraisal terms and what happens if it comes in low
  • Closing date, possession, and who carries which cost
06

Contract to close, tracked

A dedicated transaction coordinator manages the file: every deadline calendared, repairs negotiated, appraisal and title coordinated, and your closing figures reviewed with you before closing day.
